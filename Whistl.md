# Privacy Policy for Whistl

**Last Updated:** November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Whistl** ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data accessed or processed by our application.

By using Whistl, you agree to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy.

---

### 1. Overview & Core Philosophy
Whistl is a kitchen utility tool designed to count cooker whistles using smart on-device sound detection. **Your privacy is our priority:** all audio detection and machine learning analysis is performed **100% locally on your device**. We do not record your conversations, and we do not upload any audio data to the internet.

---

### 2. Information We Access & How It Is Used

#### A. Microphone & Audio Data (`RECORD_AUDIO`)
* **Purpose:** The app requires access to your device's microphone solely to listen for and count pressure cooker whistle sounds in real-time.
* **On-Device Processing:** Audio streams are processed in temporary RAM buffers using a local, on-device machine learning model (TensorFlow Lite).
* **No Storage & No Transmission:** Audio data is **never recorded to persistent storage, never saved as audio files, and never transmitted over the internet or to external servers**. Once a sound buffer is analyzed, it is immediately discarded.

#### B. Notifications & Background Service (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE`)
* **Purpose:** Used strictly to display a persistent notification showing your live whistle count and to ring the completion alarm when your cooker reaches the target whistle count, even when the screen is locked or the app is minimized.

#### C. Internet & Network State (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)
* **Purpose:** Required solely for displaying mobile advertisements through Google AdMob when your device is connected to the internet. Whistl’s core whistle detection works entirely offline without internet connectivity.
